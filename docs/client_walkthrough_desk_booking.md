# Desk Booking: a best-practice guide for office admins

*For the person who sets up and looks after desk booking in your offices. About 10 minutes to read.*

> **About the numbers:** the benchmarks below come from a **synthetic** portfolio of 40 clients built for a portfolio project, not from real customers. Treat them as examples of the kind of checks to run. The practices themselves (checklist, messages) are recommendations, not measured results.

---

## 1. What a healthy setup looks like

Across the 39 clients in the sample that use Desk Booking:

| Check | Typical client | Worth a closer look if |
|---|---|---|
| Employees who booked a desk in the last 90 days | **31%** (range 12.5% to 52.3%) | Well under 15% a quarter after launch |
| Desk views that turn into a booking | **75%** (the middle client) | Fewer than about 4 in 10 |
| Bookings later cancelled | **about 1 in 10** (9.9%) | A sudden jump |
| Bookings where the person did not turn up | **5.5%** | Roughly double that |
| People who booked one month and booked again the next (first-half-year launches) | **80% to 85%** | Falling month after month |
| Bookings made Tuesday to Thursday | **69%** | Not a problem by itself, but plan desk numbers around it |

## 2. Before you switch it on

- [ ] **Desks are listed for every office** (floors, zones, numbers) and match the real floor.
- [ ] **Decide the rule people will live with** (section 3) and write it in one sentence.
- [ ] **Turn it on for all your offices, or fix the dates for each one.** A client that switches on 1 office out of 4 leaves most employees with nothing to use. If you stagger, publish the dates.
- [ ] **If you plan QR check-in, install the scanners first.** Check-in without scanners tends to be tried once and dropped.
- [ ] **Name a person** who answers "how do I book?" for the first month.

## 3. Setting the attendance rule

The rule is the most common cause of "people look but don't book".

- Start with **3 office days per week or more** unless there is a real space limit. A cap of 1 day a week meant only about 14 to 16 in 100 desk views became a booking in our sample.
- If you must cap, **allow manager exceptions** and say how to ask.
- After the first two weeks, check how many people **opened the desk map but did not book**. If it is more than half, look at the rule before anything else.

## 4. The first 30 days: nudges that help

Send three short messages. Keep them in plain words and say what to do.

1. **Day 0 (launch):** "From Monday, book your desk in the app before you come in. It takes under a minute. Questions: [name], [contact]."
2. **Day 7:** "Most people book Tuesday to Thursday. If you are in on those days, book early for your preferred zone."
3. **Day 21:** "Can't come in? Cancel your booking so a colleague can use the desk."

Also: ask team leads to book as a team on the same days, and put a QR poster at the entrance that opens the booking page.

## 5. No-shows and cancellations

- Ask people to **check in**. Check-in is what lets you release desks that nobody turned up for.
- If no-shows are roughly double the typical 5.5%, **release unclaimed desks after an agreed time** (for example 10 am) and tell people the rule.
- Cancellations are normal (about 1 in 10). A sharp rise usually means plans changed, such as a holiday or a policy change.

## 6. Your monthly 5-minute check

Look at five numbers and compare with the table in section 1:

1. Employees who booked in the last 90 days
2. Views that became bookings
3. Cancellation rate
4. No-show rate
5. How many offices have Desk Booking switched on

Write them down each month. The trend matters more than any single month. Remember that **late December and festival weeks (such as Diwali) are always quieter**. Compare with the same weeks last year or with other offices, not with the month before.

## 7. When to contact your account manager

- Fewer than about 4 in 10 desk views turn into bookings
- Usage drops by more than half over a few weeks and support tickets rise at the same time
- Employees in some offices tell you they have no way to book
- QR check-in is used heavily in the first month and then almost stops
- Parking or room booking is switched on but cannot be used because spots, rooms or calendar sync are not set up
